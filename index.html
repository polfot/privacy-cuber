Cuber Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Cuber is an offline game. It does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Cuber is made by Strada Minigames ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Cuber app for Android and iOS handles information.

Information we collect

None. Cuber has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking tools. The game does not connect to the internet while you play.

What is saved on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the game keeps a few values in the app's own storage on your device:

  • your best score in Endless mode,
  • the highest Story level you have unlocked,
  • your settings (volume, sound effects, vibration, particles).

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, removes it.

Device features

Cuber can use your device's vibration motor for haptic feedback. You can turn this off in the game's Settings. No information is gathered through it.

Children

Cuber does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Sharing

Because we collect no information, we have nothing to share, sell or transfer to anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Cuber changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
